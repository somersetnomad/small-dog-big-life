Would a Chihuahua suit us? Meet the breed
Editor’s note

They weigh about as much as a bag of sugar. They don’t know that.

A Chihuahua is a whole dog in a very small body: alert, opinionated, devoted, and more fragile than its confidence suggests. Living well with one means taking its size seriously without letting its size be the whole story.

  1. i.Teeth before trendsDental disease was the most commonly recorded condition in UK Chihuahuas in veterinary records.
  2. ii.Confidence, not carryingFour paws on the ground builds a braver dog.
  3. iii.How a puppy beganPopular small breeds attract poor breeding. Know the signs.

If something is wrong now

Don’t read guides. Phone your vet, or the out-of-hours service on your vet’s answerphone.

Phone straight away for breathing difficulty, collapse, a seizure, a puppy who is weak or trembling and won’t eat, suspected poisoning, heatstroke, or a fall from a height.
